Mile Marker 1 — Getting Settled

House Guide

Quick instructions for the essentials around the apartment.

AC & Heat

Thermostat is on the hallway wall by the kitchen. Press the dial to adjust — please keep it between 68–76°F while the windows are open so it doesn't work overtime.

Kitchen

Coffee, tea, and basic spices are stocked in the cabinet above the coffee maker. Dishwasher pods are under the sink — just push start, no rinse needed.

TV & Streaming

Netflix and YouTube are logged in on the living room TV. Use the black remote — press "Input" if it opens to the wrong source.

Parking

One assigned spot in the lot behind the building, marked with your unit number. Street parking is free after 6pm and on weekends.

Trash & Recycling

Bins are in the side alley. Trash pickup is Tuesday and Friday mornings — please have bags out by 8am.

Beach Gear

Umbrellas, chairs, and towels are in the hall closet. Please rinse and hang towels to dry on the back patio before your stay ends.

House Rules

  • Quiet hours are 10pm–8am, please be mindful of neighbors
  • No smoking inside the apartment
  • No additional guests beyond those booked
  • Please don't move furniture between rooms
Mile Marker 2 — Before You Go

Check-out Instructions

Check-out time 10:00 AM
1

Start the dishwasher — load any dishes in the sink and press start, no need to unload it.

2

Take out the trash — bag it up and drop it in the alley bins out back.

3

Gather towels — leave used towels and linens in a pile by the washing machine.

4

Turn off AC/heat and lights — set the thermostat back to 72°F.

5

Lock up — pull the front door shut until it clicks, and drop the key in the lockbox by the entrance.

Need a later check-out? Text your host before 9am the day of and we'll do our best to accommodate.
